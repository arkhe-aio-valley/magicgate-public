## Vision

MagicGate is a plan-driven technology execution platform designed to reduce the operational work required between software intent and a validated delivery.

Its central thesis is:

> Software complexity should not require proportional growth in the amount of human work needed to build, validate, and evolve it.

MagicGate is designed to expand the operational capacity of engineering professionals and teams through controlled execution, measurement, evidence, and governance.

Human professionals remain responsible for objectives, decisions, architecture, context, validation, and judgment.

---

## The problem

Building and evolving complex software still consumes a large amount of human attention.

Between an idea and a real delivery there are planning, implementation, testing, diagnosis, validation, integration, operations, and evolution tasks.

Engineering cost is not only the cost of producing code. It is also the amount of human work required to turn intent into functional, validated, operational software.

> The economic problem of software development is not only producing code. It is safely reducing the human work required to produce verifiable technology outcomes.

---

## The MagicGate proposition

MagicGate turns structured plans into controlled operational flows.

Its purpose is to progressively reduce repetitive operational work that can be executed safely while keeping critical decisions and judgment under human responsibility.

Conceptually, the product cycle is:

**define → execute → test → validate → record → measure → evolve**

The goal is to move professionals away from permanently executing microtasks and toward supervising and orchestrating technological capacity.

## Metrics

MagicGate was designed to measure its own operation.

Observable indicators may include:

- execution duration;
- autonomy level;
- need for human intervention;
- test results;
- quality gates;
- retries;
- delivery evidence;
- productivity;
- operating cost;
- human-equivalent estimates when explicitly identified as such.

### Validation rule

A metric may be publicly classified as **validated** only when it is derived from a test that was actually executed and has sufficient evidence to support the result.

**Executed test + observable evidence = validated metric**

Estimates, projections, hypotheses, targets, conceptual scenarios, and theoretical references are not validated metrics.

---

## MagicGate Productivity Index

The **MGPI, MagicGate Productivity Index**, consolidates multiple operational dimensions to support a consistent view of productivity, autonomy, quality, and evidence.

An MGPI value is presented as observed or validated only when its source data comes from real, properly recorded executions.

> Productivity should be demonstrated by evidence, not merely by perception.

## Security, privacy, and governance

MagicGate publicly adopts a safe-stop preference when an execution cannot be validated adequately.

Public principles include:

- controlled execution;
- safe behavior under ambiguity;
- protection against destructive actions;
- confinement to authorized context;
- blocking material that appears to contain secrets;
- evidence requirements for completion;
- separation between automation and decisions that require human judgment;
- public disclosure only after appropriate approval;
- data minimization in future commercial telemetry;
- no default collection of source code, credentials, complete prompts, or proprietary client content in the planned commercial layer.

For future device-based trial control, the project plans to use a **pseudonymous Device ID and installation cryptographic key**, rather than storing a raw MAC address.

> Automation without governance is not sustainable scale.
